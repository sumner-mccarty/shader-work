# Look backlog

Each brief is a starting point for one look (dark + light). A worker may push it further but must
keep the **signature** — the one thing that makes it recognisable in a second. Class: `lit` = raymarched
+ lamp rig (premium), `unlit` = flat shaders, `_LightingUnlit 1` (phone-cheap), `neon` = unlit tubes
(Tron template). Patterns/shapes named here exist in the shaders today (Docs/Skinning/Params-*.md).

**Never** use a real brand, artist, team or product name, logo or trade dress — evoke the era/genre.

## Wave 1 — buildable now, strongest variety (start here)

### gold-leaf — Gold Leaf · lit
Black lacquer plates (Plastic, high spec, low rough) with 24k gold hardware: knurled gold knob skirts
(Knurled), domed gold caps (RadialBrushed), gold-edged keys. Warm key light, champagne specular.
Light mode: ivory lacquer chassis, same gold. Signature: gold that reads as METAL (dome + warm spec
gradient), never as yellow plastic. Accent: deep emerald for ON.

### liquid-glass — Liquid Glass · lit
Water and glass: frosted pale-aqua plates (Frosted, translucent body alpha ~0.85 over a deep-teal
backdrop), clear domed "droplet" buttons with a bright spec rim and a darker refractive core
(radial gradient), knobs as glass pucks with a caustic shimmer (NoiseOrganic, slow pattern speed).
Light mode: sea-glass white. Signature: everything looks wet. (Wave 3 adds true reflection.)

### rodeo — Country & Western · lit
Tooled saddle-leather faceplates (Leather, warm tan → oxblood gradient), stitched edges (dashed
outer ring/border), silver concho knobs (Fluted skirt, engraved RadialBrushed cap) with turquoise
inlay caps, pads like branded leather patches. Lamps = amber oil glow. Light mode: bleached rawhide
and pale denim. Signature: leather + silver + turquoise.

### honky-tonk — Honky-Tonk Neon · neon
A roadhouse at midnight: neon tubes in pink, mint and amber over dark weathered wood (WoodGrain,
very low intensity, behind tubes), chickenhead dial pointers as bent neon. Light mode: daytime sign
in sun-bleached paint with unlit glass tubes. Signature: warm bar neon on wood, not cyber-blue.

### ice-cold — Ice Cold (hip-hop) · lit
Matte black chassis, heavy gold chain detail: knurled thick gold knob skirts, gold bezel rings,
"iced" accents — keys and lamps with a sparkling white-diamond face (high-scale Ceramic + strong
spec + glow). Light mode: white leather chassis (Leather) with the same gold and ice. Signature:
flex — gold mass + diamond sparkle.

### boom-box — Boom Box '86 · lit
Chrome-and-black 80s portable stereo: brushed chrome plates (Metal), speaker-grille wells
(Perforated), chunky square keys with coloured caps (red/yellow/blue/green), slider faders with
ridged caps, VU-green lamps. Light mode: silver-grey plastic chassis. Signature: grille + chunky keys.

### graffiti — Street Wall · unlit
Concrete plates (Concrete) with spray-paint gradients (hot magenta→cyan, lime→orange) on keys and
knob arcs, thick black outlines like marker strokes, drips implied by vertical gradients. Light mode:
whitewashed wall, same paint. Signature: raw concrete + loud spray colour, all flat.

### candy-cane — Candy Cane Christmas · lit
Red velvet plates (Fabric) with gold trim, candy-cane striped slider tracks and knob skirts (repeating
triangle-gradient bands, red/white), pine-green keys, warm fairy-light lamps (amber glow, slightly
uneven per lamp). Light mode: snow-white frosted chassis (Frosted) with red and green.
Signature: stripes + velvet + warm twinkle. Ship it as seasonal.

### walnut-brass — Walnut & Brass Hi-Fi · lit
70s hi-fi receiver: oiled walnut side plates and inset panels (WoodGrain, px-locked), brushed brass
faceplate (Metal, warm tint), skirted aluminium knobs with fine knurl, cream dial lamps behind amber
glass. Light mode: teak + champagne aluminium. Signature: real wood + warm metal.

### synthwave — Outrun · neon
Sunset gradient plates (deep purple → magenta → orange), grid perspective feel via HexGrid/Circuit at
low intensity, chrome-cyan tubes, magenta ON. Light mode: pastel vaporwave (lilac, mint, peach,
unlit). Signature: sunset gradient + grid.

### marble-rose — Marble & Rose Gold · lit
White Carrara marble plates (Marble, grey veins), rose-gold domed hardware, blush-pink accents,
soft daylight rig. Dark mode: Nero Marquina black marble with gold veins (Marble + pattern colour).
Signature: stone + jewellery.

### carbon-race — Carbon Race · lit
Carbon-fibre plates (CarbonFiber), red anodised knob skirts, machined aluminium collets (Collet
shape), yellow warning lamps, racing-stripe on the backplane (gradient band). Light mode: white
ceramic-composite chassis (Ceramic). Signature: carbon weave + red anodised.

## Making more — the theme formula

When the list runs out, combine one from each axis and write a new brief in this file first:

* **World:** a genre, era, season, place, craft, or object ("1950s diner", "Nordic winter",
  "vinyl record shop", "Moroccan tile", "space station", "sushi bar", "skate park")
* **Primary material** (one of the 20 patterns + metal/plastic/glass/neon) and **secondary material**
* **Form language:** square/soft/round corners, flat/domed/chamfered, knob silhouette (22 shapes)
* **Lighting class:** lit / unlit / neon
* **Palette structure:** dark-on-light or light-on-dark, warm or cool, one accent or many

A new brief must differ from every existing look in at least two axes (Looks/README.md rubric #1).
Colourways (palette-only variants of an approved look) multiply the catalogue further without new
structure — see FACTORY.md Phase 4.
