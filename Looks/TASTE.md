## What the owner has said so far
* "Most of the skins feel okay, not stellar or amazing at all." — competent is not the target.
* Liquid Glass must look like the modern translucent-glass UI style: mostly transparent/translucent
  parts showing what is behind them — not tinted plastic.
* Realistic Dark (~90%) beats the other shipped looks (~50%) — physical conviction wins.

## Patterns the owner rejected
* flat "sticker" metal (no moving highlight); camouflage-blotch marble; dark controls lost on dark
  plates; unreadable print on light chassis; invisible hover on pads.
